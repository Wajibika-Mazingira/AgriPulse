# Rights and reuse

No repository-wide open licence is asserted. The manuscript and original analysis remain subject to their authors’ rights; permission for reuse should be obtained from the relevant rights holder.

Third-party material retains its own terms. In particular:

- Sentinel-2 imagery is Copernicus data and is provided under the Copernicus Sentinel data terms; scene IDs and derived-crop metadata are included in `visuals/data/imagery_scenes.csv`.
- Kenya county boundaries are from geoBoundaries Kenya ADM1 (2020), identified as public-domain data by the provider.
- OpenStreetMap-derived place references are subject to the OpenStreetMap ODbL; attribution and coordinate limits are documented in the visualisation README and site registry.
- The repository cites judgments, news reporting, research and agency plans. Their contents are not relicensed here.

The `data` directory contains source-traceable research tables and derived graph/map inputs, not a licence to reuse confidential community knowledge. Sensitive or restricted Kaya knowledge and private farm coordinates are intentionally omitted.
